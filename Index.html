Hifz Program

Dedicated to fostering a strong connection with the Holy Qur'an. Producing knowledgeable Huffaz who are protectors of the Book and successful in life.

About the Program

Al Bayan Hifz Program

The program is led by highly qualified and certified Qur'an teachers who hold an ijazah in the Holy Qur'an. Students are regularly assessed through Juz exams and their progress is closely monitored.

The program is open to both new and continuing students. All applicants are assessed by a certified ijazah teacher to determine proficiency in tajweed and level of Qur'anic memorization. Part-Time Hifz option now available.

✓Small student-teacher ratio
✓Effective memorization & retention techniques
✓Individual and group revision sessions
✓Ongoing academic education support
✓Al-Qaida Noorania, Tajweed & Qira'at
✓Part-Time Hifz option available

Kindergarten

Full-time Montessori Kindergarten equipping 5-year-olds for bright futures through faith, success, and leadership.

About Kindergarten

Al Bayan Kindergarten

The Kindergarten Program is a full-time program for 5-year-olds. In a Montessori setting, students receive a comprehensive education encompassing Language Arts, Math, Science, Social Studies, Qur'an, Arabic, and Islamic Studies.

Along with academic subjects, practical life skills are integrated into the curriculum. Our ultimate goal is to equip each child for a bright future by fostering faith, success, and leadership.

Admission:

Children must be 5 years old by September 30th.

Weekend Islamic School

Qur'an, Arabic & Islamic Studies

The Weekend Islamic School at Al Bayan offers a nurturing and structured Islamic learning environment for children ages 3–16. Held every Saturday from 10:30 AM to 2:30 PM in Norcross, GA, our program welcomes both boys and girls from across the Greater Atlanta Muslim community.

Our mission is to foster a deep love of Islam, the Qur'an, and the Arabic language in a welcoming and engaging setting that supports working families.

State Scholarship Program

Georgia Promise Scholarship

Al Bayan Learning Center is a participating school in the Georgia Promise Scholarship Program (GPS) — offering eligible students new opportunities for private education with state-funded support.

The Georgia Promise Scholarship provides eligible Georgia students with funds that can be used toward tuition and approved educational expenses at qualifying private schools like Al Bayan.

Who May Qualify?

✓Georgia residents eligible to enroll in a public school
✓Students with an Individualized Education Program (IEP)
✓Students attending a qualifying private school like Al Bayan
